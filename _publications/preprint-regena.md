excerpt: "REGENA (Robust Estimator of Gene-ENvironment Architectures) is a variance-component method that learns a phenotype transformation minimizing scale-induced gene-environment interaction and jointly estimates cross-environment genetic correlation and environment-specific genetic variances. Across 43 traits and seven exposures in 291,273 UK Biobank participants, about half of the significant GxE signals were scale-dependent; among the rest, GxE heritability averaged 15% of additive heritability and was driven mainly by effect heterogeneity."
