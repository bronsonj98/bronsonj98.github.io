excerpt: "SUMMIT combines genome-wide LD scores, estimated efficiently with random projections, with GWAS summary statistics to estimate genome-wide and annotation-specific heritability and genetic correlation. In simulations across single-ancestry and admixed cohorts it was more accurate and less biased than prior methods, with the largest gains in admixed participants; across 40 traits it recovered 26 genetic correlations at FDR < 0.05 in the two smaller cohorts, where cov-LDSC found two. The method now runs on the All of Us Research Program, an admixed cohort drawn from across the United States."
