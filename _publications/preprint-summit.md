title: "Genome-wide LD scores enable robust heritability and genetic correlation estimation from summary statistics across genetic ancestries"

excerpt: "SUMMIT combines genome-wide LD scores, estimated efficiently with random projections, with GWAS summary statistics to estimate genome-wide and annotation-specific heritability and genetic correlation. In UK Biobank simulations across European, South Asian, and admixed African-European cohorts it was more accurate and less biased than prior methods, with the largest gains outside European ancestry; across 40 traits it recovered 23 South Asian and 3 African-ancestry genetic correlations at FDR < 0.05, where cov-LDSC found none and two."
