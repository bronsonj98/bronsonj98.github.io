An introduction to probabilistic models in genomics, with emphasis on developing new computational and statistical techniques for genomic data; no prior background in biology is required. The course was formerly numbered CM121/CM221 (Introduction to Bioinformatics). C121 is concurrently scheduled with the graduate-level C221.
