Platform (oral) presentation at the American Society of Human Genetics (ASHG) 2026 Annual Meeting in Montréal on Thursday, October 22, 2026, in the platform session "From Labels to Landscapes: Leveraging Local Ancestry and the Ancestry Continuum to Maximize Cross-Ancestry Association". The talk presented SUMMIT, the genome-wide LD score approach to heritability and genetic correlation described in the [bioRxiv preprint](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.20.760508).
